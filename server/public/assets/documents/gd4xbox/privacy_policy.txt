Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 23rd, 2026

Nogitsune Development ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Google Drive for Xbox application
(the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.


Information We Collect

Google Drive for Xbox does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to Nogitsune
Development servers.

The App uses Google's OAuth authentication system to allow you to securely authorize access to
your Google Drive account. During this process, Google may provide the App with authorization
information required to access your account.

The App only requests the permissions necessary to provide its file browsing functionality.


Google Account and Google Drive Access

Google Drive for Xbox connects directly to your Google Drive account using Google's OAuth
authentication and Google Drive API.

Your Google account credentials are never visible to or stored by Nogitsune Development.

OAuth access tokens required for authentication are stored locally on your Xbox device and are
not transmitted to or stored by Nogitsune Development.

The App uses Google Drive access solely to provide the functionality requested by you,
including browsing and accessing files stored in your Google Drive account.

We do not:
•   Store your Google Drive files.
•   Store your Google Drive file metadata.
•   Store your OAuth tokens.
•   Access your Google Drive content for purposes unrelated to providing App functionality.
•   Sell, share, or use Google user data for advertising or marketing purposes.
•   Transfer Google user data to our own cloud infrastructure.

You may revoke the App's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google
Account security settings.


Google API Services User Data Policy

Nogitsune Development's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies
with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide or improve the functionality
of Google Drive for Xbox as requested by the user.


Data Storage

Google Drive for Xbox does not operate any servers that store user data.

Any authentication information required for the operation of the App is stored locally on your
Xbox device.

Nogitsune Development does not maintain databases containing user information, Google Drive
content, authentication credentials, or usage history.


Third-Party Services

The App relies on third-party services, including:
•   Google OAuth
•   Google Drive API
•   Microsoft Xbox platform services

These services are operated independently by their respective providers. Your use of these
services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Nogitsune Development does not control how third-party providers collect, use, or process
information outside of the App.


Children's Privacy

Google Drive for Xbox does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the App does not collect or store personal information, we do not maintain personal data
about users of any age.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us unintentionally, please contact
us so that we can address the concern.


Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the App and ensure that authentication information is
handled securely.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.


Changes to This Privacy Policy

Nogitsune Development may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be posted with a revised "Last Updated"
date. Where appropriate, additional notice may be provided through the App or other available

Your continued use of the App after changes become effective indicates acceptance of the

Your Rights and Choices

You may stop using the App at any time by uninstalling it from your Xbox device.

You may revoke Google Drive access granted to the App through your Google Account security
settings.

Because Nogitsune Development does not collect or store personal information, there may be
